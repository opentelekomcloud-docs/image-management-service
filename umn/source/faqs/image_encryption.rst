Image Encryption
================

How Can I Change an Unencrypted Image to an Encrypted One?
----------------------------------------------------------

You can select an encryption key when you replicate the image. Then, the system will generate an encrypted version of the unencrypted image.

.. _en-us_topic_0049196765__section460431416289:

How Do I Check the Encryption Status of a Disk in an Encrypted Full-ECS Image?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

#. Log in to the management console.
#. On the **Private Images** tab page, click an image name to go to its details page.
#. Click the **Backups** tab.
#. On the **Backup Details** page, check the disk encryption status in the **Encrypted** column.
