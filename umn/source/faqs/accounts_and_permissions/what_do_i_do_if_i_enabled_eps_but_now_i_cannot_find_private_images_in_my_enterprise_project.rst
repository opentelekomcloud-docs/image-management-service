What Do I Do If I Enabled EPS But Now I Cannot Find Private Images in My Enterprise Project?
============================================================================================

Scenarios
---------

If you cannot find the private images on the **Enterprise Project Management Service** page, add the private images to their associated enterprise project.

Procedure
---------

#. Log in to the management console.
#. Choose **Computing** > **Image Management Service**.
#. Click the **Private Images** tab.
#. Locate the row that contains the image, click **More** in the **Operation** column, and select **Allocate to Enterprise Project**.
#. In the displayed dialog box, select the target enterprise project.
