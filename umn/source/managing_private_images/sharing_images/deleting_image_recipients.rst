Deleting Image Recipients
=========================

Scenarios
---------

You can delete tenants from an image recipient list.

Prerequisites
-------------

-  You have shared private images.
-  You have obtained the project IDs of the tenants.

Procedure
---------

#. Log in to the management console.

   a. Under **Computing**, click **Image Management Service**. The IMS console is displayed.

#. Click the **Private Images** tab.

#. Click the target image name. The image details page is displayed.

#. View information about the recipients.

#. Delete one or all of the recipients.

   -  To delete a single image recipient, locate the recipient and click **Delete**.
   -  To delete all image recipients, click **Delete All** above the image recipient list.

#. Click **OK**.
